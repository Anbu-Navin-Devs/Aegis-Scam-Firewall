## 💡 Executive Summary

Consumer mobile devices face a new generation of **multimodal cyber-extortion** attacks:
1. **Generative Smishing**: Context-aware SMS messages impersonating banks, package delivery couriers, and federal law enforcement.
2. **AI Voice Cloning**: Scammers use 3-second audio clips to clone family members' voices or generate synthetic authority figures in real-time.
3. **Predatory Legal Paperwork**: Hidden binding arbitration waivers, auto-renewals, and punitive liquidated damages concealed in consumer contracts.

Existing commercial products (Truecaller, Hiya, McAfee, Google Pixel) fail because they are **reactive, siloed, manual, and cloud-bound**. 

**Aegis** bridges these gaps by functioning as an **autonomous, on-device mobile firewall** that operates in the background with zero user friction, classifies threats in **under 2 milliseconds locally**, fuses multi-channel vectors into a single threat score, delivers live verbal countermeasure scripts to empower the victim, and generates 1-tap standardized forensic incident reports for law enforcement.
